Privacy Policy – Viral Shorts Studio
Last updated: September 27, 2026

Viral Shorts Studio ("the App") is a private tool operated by [Twoje imię i nazwisko / nazwa] for publishing short videos to the operator's own social media accounts.

1. Data we access
When you connect a TikTok account, the App receives: your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and access tokens that allow uploading videos to your account. When you connect a YouTube channel, the App receives the channel name, ID and access tokens needed to upload videos and read video statistics.

2. How we use the data
The data is used solely to publish videos you have explicitly approved and to display basic statistics about those videos. We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

3. Storage and security
All data is stored locally on the operator's computer. It is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, except to the platforms themselves (TikTok, YouTube) when publishing. Video transcription and text generation are processed by OpenAI's API; no account data is sent there.

4. Retention and deletion
Access tokens are stored until you disconnect the account. You can disconnect at any time in the App or revoke access in your TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions); after disconnecting, the stored tokens are deleted.

5. Children
The App is not intended for users under 18.
